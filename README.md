# Crowdsafe AI 🚨

## 📌 Problem Statement
Bade aayojano (jaise Kumbh Mela aur dharamik samagamon) mein lakhon-krodon logon ki behad bheed ekattha hone ki wajah se achanak bhagdad (stampede) machne ka jo khatra rehta hai, use time par rokna sabse badi chunouti hai. 

Aaj ki tarikh mein traditional methods (jaise CCTV ya manual monitoring) se real-time crowd pressure aur panic ka pata nahi chal pata, jisse samay rehte action nahi liya ja sakta aur jaan-maal ka bhari nuksan hota hai.

## 💡 Proposed Solution & Key Features
**Crowdsafe AI** ek advanced AI-powered system hai jo is problem ka smart solution provide karta hai:
* **Real-time Crowd Density Monitoring:** Bheed ke dabav (pressure) aur density ko live track karna.
* **Panic & Movement Detection:** Achanak hone wali tezi, halchal ya panic ko pehle hi detect karna.
* **Early Warning System:** Authorities aur security personnel ko instant alerts bhejna taaki samay rehte bheed ko control kiya ja sake.
* **Smart Dashboard:** Traditional CCTV monitoring ki kamiyon ko door karke automated insights aur heatmaps dena.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack / Approach
* **AI & Computer Vision Models:** Live video feeds ko analyze karne ke liye.
* **Density Mapping Algorithms:** Bheed ke concentration aur pressure ko map karne ke liye.
* **Instant Alert System:** SMS/App notifications ke zariye authorities ko turant soochit karne ke liye.

## 🎯 Project Impact
Is system ka mukhya uddeshya bade aayojano mein hone wale achanak hadson ko rokna, safety management ko behtar banana aur hazaron-laakhon logon ki jaan bachana hai.
